# Prompt para preparar Speaking e Listening de inglês

A gramática não entra aqui: ela é estudada com os três livros, num Projeto do Claude (prompts do próprio app).

Cole o texto abaixo numa conversa com outro agente. A resposta vem no formato de importação do app: em Capacidades (ou Projetos) → Importar arquivo.
A linha `> Times: N` vira o campo "Vezes para trabalhar" da parte.

```
I am a Brazilian learning English for work (I am a data professional). Help me prepare my English study: Speaking and Listening. Answer in simple English.

How I learn:
- Active recall: I explain from scratch, in my own words, as to a beginner, with examples. Then you tell me what I forgot or got wrong, I fix it with questions, and I explain again until it is right.
- Small parts. Each part is one topic. I practice each part a set number of times, and I mark it as finished only when I can really do it (speak about it without help, or understand it when listening).
- I keep notes in markdown and review cards (questions I answer from memory), with spaced reviews.
- I study at home at night, on a tablet. Speaking: a voice chat with an AI at lunch. Listening: at night, in 4 passes: without subtitles, with English subtitles (I look up what I missed), again without subtitles, and then I tell the AI what it was about.

What I need from you:
1. First ask me up to 5 short questions (my work, my level, what I must say or understand at work). Wait for my answers.
2. Then make two lists:
   A) English Speaking: 30 topics I need to be able to speak about (work, data, meetings, daily life), most useful first. For each one: the topic written as "I can talk about ...", 3 key phrases, and how many times to practice it (3 to 7).
   B) English Listening: 30 things I need to be able to understand (for example a tech talk on a subject, a meeting, a podcast, a technical explanation), most useful first. For each one: what to listen to (the type of content and what to search for), what I must understand, and how many times to practice it (3 to 7).
3. Keep your messages short and simple. No long explanations.

Write the lists in this format, so I can import them into my app (one block per list):

CAPABILITY: English Speaking
AREA: Professional
- Talk about my job
  > Times: 5
  > Key phrases: I work as ...; My main task is ...

CAPABILITY: English Listening
AREA: Professional
- Understand a tech talk about data quality
  > Times: 4
  > Listen to: a conference talk on data quality. Understand: the main problem and two solutions.


```
